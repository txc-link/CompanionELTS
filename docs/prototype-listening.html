⚖️ 错题溯源 · 第 22 题

题目：What is the main topic of the presentation?

你的答案：B (Economic impact) — ✘ 错误

正确答案：C (Environmental impact)

💡 解析：原文中说话者明确提到 "I think we should concentrate on the environmental impact rather than the economic aspect"。这是典型的同义替换考点：concentrate on ≈ focus on / primary focus。选项 B 是干扰项 — 说话者确实提到了 economic aspect，但用了 "rather than" 表示否定。

📚 相关考点：否定信号词 (rather than, instead of) + 观点转折 (but, however) 后的内容往往是正确答案。

📝 同类词汇：concentrate on, focus on, center on, revolve around, be concerned with
