语法
原文 the government should take measures to protecting the environment 建议 the government should take measures to protect the environment
说明：介词 "to" 在此处是不定式标记，后接动词原形，不应使用动名词 "protecting"。类似错误在文中出现两次，建议统一修正。
优化
原文 Many people think that personal efforts are negligible compared to industrial pollution 建议 A considerable number of individuals contend that personal efforts pale in significance when set against industrial pollution
说明："Many people think" 过于口语化。改用更学术化的表达可提升 Lexical Resource (LR) 得分。同时 "negligible" 可替换为更生动的短语 "pale in significance"。
替换
原文 which encourage people to make good choices that reduce pollution 建议 which encourage people to make judicious choices that mitigate pollution
说明："good" 在学术写作中过于笼统且频次过高。建议替换为 "judicious"（审慎的）或 "sustainable"（可持续的）；"reduce" 可升级为 "mitigate" 以体现词汇多样性。
