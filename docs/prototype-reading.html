Question 1 of 13
The government's policy on renewable energy has been successful in reducing carbon emissions.
❌ 你的答案: TRUE ✅ 正确答案: FALSE
解析：原文指出该政策仅"partially successful"（部分成功），排放"decreased by only 2%"（仅减少2%），远未达到"successful"的程度，因此选FALSE。
📍 关键定位: "...the policy was deemed partially successful, as emissions decreased by only 2% over the five-year period..."
📄 段落预览

In recent years, the government has implemented a series of policies aimed at promoting renewable energy adoption across the industrial sector. These initiatives included tax incentives for solar panel installation, subsidies for wind farm development, and grants for research into next-generation battery storage technology. However, a comprehensive review published last month revealed that the policy was deemed partially successful, as emissions decreased by only 2% over the five-year period. Critics argue that the targets were not ambitious enough...
